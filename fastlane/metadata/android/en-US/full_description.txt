Niva Launcher is a free and open-source Android launcher with a clean, list-based home screen.

• Keep your favorite apps close and browse with the alphabet rail.
• View upcoming calendar events and notifications.
• Control your music from the home screen.
• Customize colors, icons and folders with Material You.
• See the weather with Breezy Weather, installed and configured separately.

No ads or tracking. Niva Launcher does not request Internet access. Calendar and notification access are optional and enable the corresponding features.

Requires Android 9 or later.
